Day/night mode

The same controls as under the live picture. Auto follows the light level with the thresholds below; Day and Night fix the mode until you change it.

Automatic switching thresholds

Used in Auto mode. Night starts when the light falls below the first value; day comes back above the second one (the gap avoids flapping at dusk).

The picture luminance stays near the auto-exposure target whatever the light, and rises when the infrared lights come on. The exposure index (exposure time times the gains) measures the scene light itself; the dark flag is the image processor's own decision. If the exposure data is missing, the luminance is used.

Firmware update

Firmware 3.0 (Linux 6.6) running from the flash memory: upload tc100-<version>-update.tar from the release page; the system, kernel and boot image are written to the flash, read back, and the camera restarts. Settings, Wi-Fi and the camera's own libraries are kept.

Writes the 3.0 firmware of this card into the camera's own memory: the free system, and a copy of the camera's own libraries (taken from this camera, never published). Each partition is saved to tc100-3/backup on the card first and read back after writing. At the end the camera restarts from its flash; the card is then only used for recordings.
